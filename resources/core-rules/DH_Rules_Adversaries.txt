ADVERSARIES AND ENVIRONMENTS

================================================================================
ADVERSARY STAT BLOCKS
================================================================================

All information required to run an adversary is contained in their stat block:

NAME: Unique identifier. Abilities affecting adversaries with a certain name include all adversaries using that stat block.

TIER: Designed to oppose PCs of a certain tier. Adjust stats if using adversaries from different tiers.

TYPE: The role they play in conflict:
- BRUISERS: Tough; deliver powerful attacks
- HORDES: Groups of identical creatures acting together as a single unit
- LEADERS: Command and summon other adversaries
- MINIONS: Easily dispatched but dangerous in numbers
- RANGED: Fragile in close encounters but deal high damage at range
- SKULKS: Maneuver and exploit opportunities to ambush opponents
- SOCIALS: Present challenges around conversation instead of combat
- SOLOS: Present a formidable challenge to a whole party
- STANDARDS: Representative of their fictional group
- SUPPORTS: Enhance their allies and disrupt their opponents

DESCRIPTION: Narrative description of the adversary

MOTIVES & TACTICS: Goals and combat behaviors

DIFFICULTY: The Difficulty of any roll made against the adversary

DAMAGE THRESHOLDS, HIT POINTS, AND STRESS: Function the same as for PCs. Listed as "Threshold: Major/Severe"

ATTACK MODIFIER: Bonus or penalty applied to attack rolls

STANDARD ATTACK: Primary mode of inflicting harm (name, range, damage). Using this is a GM move.

EXPERIENCE (OPTIONAL): GM can spend Fear to add to attack roll or increase Difficulty of rolls against them.

FEATURES: Three kinds:
- ACTIONS: Special attack or unique action when spotlighted
- REACTIONS: Effects that trigger regardless of spotlight
- PASSIVES: Abilities that remain in effect by default

FEAR FEATURES: High-impact effects that cost a Fear to activate.

================================================================================
EXAMPLE ADVERSARY FEATURES
================================================================================

ACTIONS:
- Haymaker: Make an attack against a target within Very Close range. On success, deal X direct physical damage.
- Shredding Strike: Make an attack within Very Close range. On success, deal X physical damage and target must mark an Armor Slot without gaining its benefit.
- More Where That Came From: Summon additional adversaries who appear at Far range.

REACTIONS:
- Heavy Hitter: When this adversary deals damage with a standard attack, spend a Fear to gain +X bonus to damage.
- Team-Up: When another adversary within Very Close range deals X damage, mark a Stress to make a standard attack against that same creature.
- Momentum: When this adversary makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.

PASSIVES:
- Horde (X): When the Horde has marked half or more HP, standard attack deals X damage instead.
- Minion (X): Defeated when taking any damage. For every X damage dealt, defeat an additional Minion in range.
- Relentless (X): Can be spotlighted up to X times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual.
- Slow: Can't act when first spotlighted. Place a token. On next spotlight, clear token and act.
- Arcane Form: Resistant to magic damage.
- Armored Carapace: When taking physical damage, reduce it by X.

FEAR FEATURES:
- Explosion: Spend a Fear to erupt in a fiery explosion. Make an attack against all targets within Close range. Targets take 1d8 magic damage and are knocked back to Far range.

================================================================================
BUILDING BALANCED ENCOUNTERS
================================================================================

Start with: [(3 x number of PCs in combat) + 2] Battle Points

ADJUSTMENTS:
- -1 for an easier or shorter fight
- -2 if using 2+ Solo adversaries
- -2 if adding +1d4 (or static +2) to all adversaries' damage rolls
- +1 if choosing an adversary from a lower tier
- +1 if not including any Bruisers, Hordes, Leaders, or Solos
- +2 for a harder or longer fight

SPENDING BATTLE POINTS:
- 1 point: Each Social or Support adversary
- 1 point: Each group of Minions equal to party size
- 2 points: Each Horde, Ranged, Skulk, or Standard adversary
- 3 points: Each Leader adversary
- 4 points: Each Bruiser adversary
- 5 points: Each Solo adversary

DEFEATED ADVERSARIES:
When an adversary marks their last Hit Point, they are defeated: incapacitated, tied up, routed, killed, or anything else the table decides makes sense.

================================================================================
COUNTDOWNS
================================================================================

Countdowns represent a period of time or series of events preceding a future effect. Begins at a starting value and advances by 1 until reaching 0, which triggers the effect.

STANDARD COUNTDOWNS: Advance every time a player makes an action roll. "Countdown [n]" means standard countdown with starting value of n.

DYNAMIC COUNTDOWNS: Advance by up to 3 depending on action roll outcomes.

| Roll Result        | Progress Countdown | Consequence Countdown |
|--------------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Failure with Fear  | No advancement     | Tick down 3           |
| Failure with Hope  | No advancement     | Tick down 2           |
| Success with Fear  | Tick down 1        | Tick down 1           |
| Success with Hope  | Tick down 2        | No advancement        |
| Critical Success   | Tick down 3        | No advancement        |

ADVANCED COUNTDOWN FEATURES:
- Randomized starting values
- Loop countdowns that reset after triggering
- Increasing/Decreasing countdowns that change starting value each loop
- Linked progress and consequence countdowns
- Long-term countdowns that advance after rests instead of action rolls

================================================================================
OPTIONAL GM MECHANICS
================================================================================

FATE ROLLS:
When leaving an outcome entirely up to chance, establish stakes and have a player roll one of their Duality Dice and interpret the result.

FALLING AND COLLISION DAMAGE:
- Very Close range fall: 1d10+3 physical damage
- Close range fall: 1d20+5 physical damage
- Far or Very Far range fall: 1d100+15 physical damage, or death at GM's discretion
- Collision at dangerous speed: 1d20+5 direct physical damage

MOVING AND FIGHTING UNDERWATER:
- Attack rolls while underwater have disadvantage
- For creatures that can't breathe underwater: Use standard countdown (3) to track breath
- Advance countdown when PC takes action; GM can advance extra on failed rolls
- After countdown ends, PC marks Stress for each action

CONFLICT BETWEEN PCS:
- Discuss situation with both players before rolling
- Attack roll: Attacker rolls against defender's Evasion
- Other action rolls: Instigator makes action roll, target makes reaction roll. Instigator must beat reaction roll total as Difficulty.
